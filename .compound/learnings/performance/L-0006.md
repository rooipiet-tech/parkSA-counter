statement: PostgREST/Supabase select() returns at most 1000 rows by default; an unpaginated read that feeds a dashboard, aggregation, or CSV export silently truncates past that cap with no error — correct-looking output, missing data. Any read backing analytics/export must paginate with a .range() loop until exhausted or push the filter (date range, etc.) server-side to stay under the cap. This never fails against a small stub/test dataset.
evidence: PERF-01 major (dup RS-03): SupabaseAdapter listEvents/listTombstones/listSessions used unpaginated select('*'); a one-month seed of 1216 events already exceeded 1000, silently truncating the live dashboard and CSV. Tests ran against the stub adapter and passed.
catch_next_time: Flag every PostgREST select feeding aggregation/export that lacks .range() pagination or a bounding server-side filter. Add a fixture with >1000 rows and assert the full count round-trips.
